## <span class="t-zh">后记</span><span class="t-en">Why This Blog</span>

<div class="sub-h">Why This Blog</div>

<span class="t-zh" data-pagefind-ignore>

lili 的 Blog 就这么开通了——2019 年，进京读研，总算有个地方可以好好写点东西。

作为程序员，blog 这种轮子要是挂在大众博客程序上就太没意思了。先后试过 CSDN、简书、开源中国，一是觉得太丑，二是不能随便定制——最后还是自己折腾了这个：自己写样式，自己排版。

鲁迅说：*写博客的最好时间是十年前，其次是现在。*

希望能在这里坚持写点东西，技术也好，生活也罢。借用 Steve Jobs 在斯坦福那场演讲：

</span>
<span class="t-en" data-pagefind-ignore>

And so lili's Blog went live — 2019, moving to Beijing for grad school, finally a place to write properly.

As a programmer, running a blog on some mass-market platform felt wrong. I tried CSDN, Jianshu and OSChina — either too ugly, or impossible to customize — so I ended up building my own: my own styles, my own typesetting.

Lu Xun said: *the best time to start blogging was ten years ago; the second best is now.*

I hope to keep writing here — tech or life alike. To borrow from Steve Jobs' Stanford speech:

</span>

> *"you can't connect the dots looking forward; you can only connect them looking backwards."*
